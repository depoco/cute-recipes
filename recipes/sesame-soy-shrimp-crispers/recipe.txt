Sesame Soy Shrimp Crispers

Crunchy, crispy shrimp tossed in a savory sesame soy sauce. Perfectly bite-sized and packed with flavor.

Ingredients

- 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1/2 cup cornstarch
- 1/4 cup sesame oil
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- Fresh cilantro for garnish (optional)

Instructions

1. Toss shrimp in cornstarch until evenly coated.
2. Heat sesame oil in a pan over medium-high heat.
3. Fry shrimp for 2-3 minutes per side, until crispy and golden brown.
4. In a small bowl, mix together soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, garlic powder, and red pepper flakes.
5. Once shrimp are cooked, pour the sesame soy sauce over them and toss to coat.
6. Garnish with sesame seeds and cilantro, and serve immediately.

Prep Time: 5 minutes
Cook Time: 10 minutes
Total Time: 15 minutes

Nutritional Information
- 320 kcal per serving
- Servings: 4
