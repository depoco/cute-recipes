Crispy Garlic Cod Bites with Creamy Lemon Herb Dip

Ingredients

For the cod bites:
- 1 lb cod fillets, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
- Vegetable oil, for frying

For the creamy lemon herb dip:
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp lemon zest
- 1 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions
1. In one shallow bowl, mix flour, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper.
2. In a second bowl, beat the eggs.
3. In a third bowl, add panko breadcrumbs.
4. Dredge each cod piece in flour, then egg, then panko, pressing lightly so the crumbs stick.
5. Heat about 1/2 inch of vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
6. Fry cod bites in batches for 3-4 minutes per side, until golden and crispy.
7. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate to drain.
8. Make the dip: mix mayonnaise, Greek yogurt, lemon juice, lemon zest, dill, parsley, and minced garlic. Season with salt and pepper.
9. Serve cod bites hot with the lemon herb dip.
